Where AI is used

AI assists with topic triage, structuring official-source facts, outlines, drafts, terminology consistency, links, and structured-data checks. It is not used to invent hands-on experience, measurements, customer support results, or quotations.

What requires human judgment

  • Which decision factors matter for the intended user
  • Who a service is and is not suitable for
  • Whether the conclusion remains independent of affiliate compensation
  • Whether pricing, cancellation, and migration wording could mislead
  • Final publication, updates, and corrections

How opinions are labeled

Verifiable facts and Luqevora's editorial judgment are separated. Sections labeled as editorial judgment reflect a decision framework based on cited official information and do not claim unperformed hands-on testing.
