Pricing and plan limits

Budget annual domain, hosting, production, maintenance, and analytics cost plus the operator's time—not only cash startup cost.

For affiliate marketing, identify the lowest plan containing every required feature and compare the total for the full billing period.

  • Record introductory and renewal pricing separately
  • Compare monthly, annual, and longer-term totals
  • Check limits for seats, storage, sites, sends, or usage
  • Review refund, cancellation, and data-retention terms

Strengths and trade-offs

Performance-based publishing can start without inventory, but results depend on traffic, trust, program availability, and advertiser approval.

  • Strength: Monetization can align with helping readers choose
  • Strength: Several providers can be evaluated on transparent criteria
  • Verify: Clear disclosure and accurate claims
  • Verify: Monitoring program closures, term changes, and reversals

Who it suits

It fits operators who can combine domain experience with primary research and ongoing updates.

  • Blogs with subject-matter expertise
  • Comparison and review sites
  • Operators able to test small and improve continuously

Implementation checklist

Pilot affiliate marketing in a small scope and document cost, workflow, migration, and administration before rollout.

  • Define the objective and completion criteria in one sentence
  • Reproduce a representative task in a free plan or trial
  • Test administrator, contributor, and viewer permissions
  • Perform a real backup or export test
  • Record the renewal date and internal owner
