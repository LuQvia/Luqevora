Pricing answer

How much does Semrush cost?

SEO: US$139 monthly or US$117.33/month billed annually. Starter: US$199 or US$165.17 annually. Pro+: US$299 or US$248.17 annually. Advanced: US$549 or US$455.67 annually.

SEO is the lower-cost search-focused tier. Starter and above combine SEO with broader AI Visibility allowances. Additional users, reporting products, lead-generation features and other add-ons can increase the total cost.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest current Semrush paid plan?

The SEO plan is currently listed at US$139 monthly or US$117.33 per month as the annual-billing equivalent. It includes five monitored websites and 500 tracked keywords per day.

Which Semrush plan is best for a small business?

SEO is the lower-cost choice when the priority is conventional SEO. Starter becomes relevant when AI Visibility prompt tracking is needed. Pro+ is the next step for 15 websites, 1,500 tracked keywords, history, content optimization and multi-location or device tracking.

Does Semrush have a free plan?

The current pricing page lists a free tier with one demo project and 10 reports per day across core tools. Confirm limits before relying on it for ongoing tracking.

What costs can be added to the base subscription?

Additional users start at US$45 per month on the current pricing page, and reporting or lead-generation products can also be billed separately. Price the full workflow, not only the base plan.
